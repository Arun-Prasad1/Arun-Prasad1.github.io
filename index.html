Punjab & Haryana HC Denies Bail in Narco-Terror Funding Case

January 7 2026

Synopsis The Punjab and Haryana High Court denied bail to Gurmukh Singh and Gurmej Singh, accused of smuggling narcotics, arms, and explosives from Pakistan via drones to fund terror activities in India. The court held that terror funding is an “expensive affair” sustained through drug trafficking and found prima facie evidence of the accused’s involvement, links with handlers across the border, and risk of absconding. Context The accused moved the High Court after an NIA court in Punjab rejected their bail pleas. A division bench noted the nexus between narcotics and terrorism, describing the case as an instance of narco-terrorism. The court emphasized that proceeds from heroin smuggling are used to finance terrorism at the behest of handlers based in Pakistan. The trial is ongoing, and the court dismissed claims that the case was politically motivated.
